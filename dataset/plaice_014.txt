A plaice, a type of flatfish, is camouflaged and blends seamlessly into its underwater environment, making it difficult to discern its outline or details due to its textured, colored, and shaped mimicry of the seabed. The plaice slowly glides along the ocean floor, its movement subtle and nearly imperceptible within the shifting backgrounds of sand and seagrass. The fish's body is hidden by its effective natural camouflage, making it hard to see as it merges with its surroundings.
